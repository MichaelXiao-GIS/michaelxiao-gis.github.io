01 / About

A geographic
perspective.

I’m a PhD candidate in Social and Geographic Data Science in UCL’s Department of Geography, with a background in GIS and remote sensing engineering.

I specialise in spatial data analysis and GeoAI applications. My research includes historical geodemographics, using digitised telephone directories to connect the geography of the past with contemporary questions about people and places.

Alongside my doctoral research, I work as a postgraduate teaching assistant in UCL Geography.
